Wi-Fi QR Generator

How it works

Make a QR code that lets guests join your Wi-Fi by scanning it with a phone camera. It works for WPA/WPA2, WEP and open networks, including hidden ones.

How to use it

  1. Enter the network name and choose the security type.
  2. Type the password. Open networks do not need one.
  3. Press Generate QR code, then print, download or share it.

Good to know

Is it free?
Yes. There is no account and nothing to pay.
Is my password sent anywhere?
No. The code is made in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Recent codes stay in this browser, and you can delete them at any time.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once the page has loaded you can install it and use it without a connection.
Which formats can I export?
PNG, PDF, DOCX and SVG. On phones that support it you can also share the PNG.
